Using IntelPulse

How to get in, triage an alert, read the verdict and work a case. For anything about this server itself (who may join, whether it sends email), ask its owner.

Getting in

  • Sign in with your username and password.
  • Create an account when the server's owner allows it: choose Just me for a workspace of your own, or My organization to start one your team can join.
  • Join a team from the invitation link an admin sent you. It works once, within 7 days.
  • Forgot your password? When the server sends email, Forgot your password mails a link that works once, within an hour, and signs you out everywhere. Otherwise an admin sets a new password for you, and you choose your own the next time you sign in.

Triage an alert

  1. Open the console and go to the Workbench.
  2. Paste the alert, the log lines or a list of indicators, or upload a .txt, .log, .csv or .json file of up to 5 MB.
  3. Give it a title and press Run triage, or Ctrl + Enter (⌘ + Enter on a Mac).

Private and reserved addresses are dropped on purpose. If nothing is left, the workbench says so.

Reading the verdict

ScoreVerdict
85 to 100Critical
70 to 84High
40 to 69Medium
15 to 39Low
0 to 14Informational
  • Each indicator's evidence lists every source with what it said. A source that had no key, or did not answer, is shown as such, so a quiet indicator is never mistaken for an unchecked one.
  • Why this score opens the arithmetic. Confidence is shown apart from the score: few sources agreeing is not the same as many.
  • An indicator on your workspace's allow list scores 0; one on its block list scores at least 90.
  • When an indicator was seen before, the workbench says what changed since.

Working a case

  • The Triage queue holds open cases by priority. Assign one, move it to in progress, and close it with a reason.
  • Triage history has every case, with search and filters by status, verdict, source, assignee and date.
  • The ticket downloads as Markdown or JSON, or goes to Jira or ServiceNow once an admin has set them up.
  • An admin can hold a case to keep its input past the retention period, and delete a case completely. The analyst who made a case can delete its input sooner.

For admins

  • Settings: which providers are on and their keys, Jira and ServiceNow, the retention period, and the allow and block lists.
  • Team: members and their roles, invitations, and setting a member's password.
  • API tokens: a token can read, triage, or ingest (push alerts only). It is shown once, when it is made.
  • Audit: who did what. Verify chain proves no record was edited or removed.

Pushing alerts

A SIEM or an EDR can send its alerts with an ingest token: POST /api/alerts with the alert's source, ID, title, severity and evidence. Each alert becomes a triaged case; sending the same one again returns the first case. The fields are in the API reference (opens GitHub in a new tab).

Limits

Each person and each token runs at most 30 triages a minute, and each workspace has a daily budget of triages and pushed alerts that the server's owner sets. Past either, the console says when to try again.
