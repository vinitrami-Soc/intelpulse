How it works

Paste anything.
Get the answer.

2

Privacy

Private
by design.

A SOC's alerts are its most sensitive data. IntelPulse keeps them sealed, and keeps them only as long as you say.

Day 0

Alert text sealed

The paste, the file, the pushed alert: AES-256-GCM, bound to its case.

Day 30, by default

Text deleted, verdict kept

Retention is set per workspace, 1 to 365 days. Hold a case to keep it.

An audit chain

Every change recorded, hash-chained. Verify proves nothing was edited.

Nothing from anyone else

No analytics, no third-party scripts or fonts. A strict CSP.

Keys stay sealed

Provider keys and tracker credentials, shown only by their last four characters.
